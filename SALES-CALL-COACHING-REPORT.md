# MedWave Sales Call Coaching Report
**Review period:** 14–15 April 2026
**Calls assessed:** 5
**Framework:** MedWave Six-Stage

---

## Executive Summary

Five sales calls were scored against the MedWave Six-Stage Framework (Introduction & Fit → PPP Discovery → Diagnosis & Fit Confirmation → Permission to Pitch → Value Pitch → Close). Across all five, the same five gaps appeared. They are habits, not incidents — and they are costing us deals that are otherwise ready to close.

Two prospects said **"yes" to a package verbally on the call** (James and Shikara). Neither was converted to a deposit. One prospect (Angie) quoted a price **below her own expectation** and was not closed. One prospect (Carmen) solved her own implementation blocker live on the call (*"I've got a room on my property"*) and was sent off to run a poll instead of closing.

The good news: the framework is designed to fix exactly these moments. The three highest-leverage drills are at the bottom of this report.

The framework's discovery and close mechanics are almost entirely absent. The pitch itself — credibility, case studies, product explanation — is consistently strong across all five calls. **We are losing deals at the open and at the close, not in the middle.**

## The Three Highest-Leverage Fixes

### 1. Plant the savings anchor. Every call. No exceptions.

The framework discount (R45,000 off the equipment package, R50,000 off the business package) has not been used in a single one of the five calls. It is free urgency, on-brand, and lives in our pricing structure already.

**Rule:** The first time pricing is mentioned, the savings must be mentioned in the same sentence.

**Say this:**
> *"The equipment package is R250,000 — that's normally R295,000, so you save R45,000 if we lock it in today. The business package is R295,000 — normally R345,000 — so R50,000 off. Those prices are held for seven days."*

Not "R340,000 with VAT." Not "R395,000." The number alone is a quote. The number plus the saving is a trigger.

### 2. Ask for the deposit on the call. Bundled with refundability. Every time.

The refundable-deposit ask is the framework's central close mechanic. In five calls it was properly executed zero times. Even when the prospect said "yes" to a package (James and Shikara), the deposit was not requested while the line was open.

**Rule:** If the prospect picks a package, the very next sentence is the deposit ask with refundability in the same breath.

**Say this:**
> *"Great — the business package is the right call. The 10% deposit is R34,500. It's fully refundable for seven days — if anything changes, every cent comes back. But securing it today locks your price and your install slot. I'm sending you the payment link now while we're on the line. Can you complete it while we're still on the call?"*

The payment link goes out mid-call, not after the proposal.

### 3. Run three to five minutes of real PPP discovery before any pitch.

Product-fit qualification ("do you want weight loss or pain?") is not discovery. Discovery is: **how many patients, what do they pay, what are your results now, what is the emotional cost of slow results, and why now?**

**Rule:** No pitch opens until four discovery questions have been asked and answered.

**Say this, in this order:**
1. *"Walk me through the types of patients you see in a typical week."*
2. *"What's your goal for those patients — and how long does it take to get them a real result?"*
3. *"What impact is that having on the practice — on retention, revenue, how you feel about the work?"*
4. *"Why now? Why is this the week you're looking at this?"*

Then diagnose back (Stage 3), ask permission to pitch (Stage 4), then pitch — tying every pillar back to what the prospect just told you.

## The Framework Close — Drill Script

This is the 60-second close the whole team should rehearse until it is muscle memory. Substitute the package and numbers for the prospect's chosen option.

> *"So based on what you told me about [PROSPECT'S SPECIFIC SITUATION], the business package is the right fit.*
>
> *Here's the cost: R295,000 for equipment and marketing, normally R345,000 — so you save R50,000 if we secure it today. With VAT that's R395,000, but you can claim the VAT back.*
>
> *The deposit is 10% — R39,500 — and it is fully refundable for seven days. If anything changes in the next week, every cent comes back. No questions. No friction.*
>
> *Here's what happens when we secure the deposit: I hold your price, I hold your installer slot, and my doctor starts your onboarding. If you don't pay the deposit today, I can't hold any of those three things, and the price is US-dollar-linked, so it moves.*
>
> *Which makes more sense — equipment only at R250,000 save R45,000, or the full business package at R295,000 save R50,000?*
>
> *(Listen. They'll pick one.)*
>
> *Perfect. Sending the payment link to your WhatsApp right now while we're still on the line. 30 seconds to complete. Let me know when it's through and I'll confirm your install slot."*

**What to notice:**
- Savings stated (R45K / R50K)
- Refundability stated with reassurance
- Three things locked by the deposit (price, slot, onboarding)
- Three things lost by delay (same three)
- Choice between two yeses, not yes-or-no
- Payment link goes out **while the call is still live**

## Per-Call Key Insight and One Drill Fix

### Zunaid (3/10)

**Key insight:** Zunaid told us *"business hasn't been that good either, the whole world is down"* — the single biggest buying signal of his call. We sympathised and moved on. This is a Hormozi moment: the prospect admits pain, you tie it to the product.

**Drill fix:** *"That's exactly why we're having this conversation today. A self-service cash-flow room earns whether you're in it or not. The practitioners who win in a down market are the ones who differentiate. Let's secure the R45K saving today."*

### Angie (4/10) — The one that got away

**Key insight:** Angie quoted R170K as her expectation; we came in at R201K and she said *"that's amazing, that's perfect."* That's not a negotiation line. That's a buy-mode moment. Our response was *"how heavy are you into the science?"*

**Drill fix:** When a prospect says the price is better than expected, the next sentence is the deposit ask. Not science. Not a brochure. The deposit.

### James (4/10) — Verbal yes, no deposit

**Key insight:** James said *"we'll go with the second 394 with that."* That is a yes. We pivoted to logistics and then ended the call for our own next meeting. Verbal yeses decay within 24 hours.

**Drill fix:** When a prospect picks a package, the next sentence is *"sending the payment link now while we're on the line."* Not in the proposal. Not tomorrow. Now.

### Carmen (5/10) — Room on her property

**Key insight:** In the final 90 seconds Carmen said *"I've got a room on my property, so rent is not an issue."* She solved her own biggest blocker. We said *"fantastic idea"* and hung up.

**Drill fix:** The moment a prospect removes their own friction, skip to close. *"Carmen, that changes the picture — forget the gym poll, let's launch from your property. Deposit is R39,500, refundable, I can have an installer there next Friday."*

### Shikara (6/10) — Best call, still no deposit

**Key insight:** Shikara wants a June launch and picked the business package verbally. We had perfect urgency (June installer slots fill by mid-April) and did not use it. Also: no savings anchor despite pension-funded buyer who would value R50K saving heavily.

**Drill fix:** *"Doctor, for June you need to be on my installer calendar by mid-April. R34,500 refundable deposit today holds June 12 as your install date. Without it I cannot hold the slot. Shall we do it on the call?"*

## Pre-Call Checklist (Print and Keep by the Desk)

Before the call:
- [ ] Prospect name, business, city, referral source
- [ ] Three discovery questions written out for this specific prospect
- [ ] Savings numbers for the two packages rehearsed (R45K / R50K)
- [ ] Payment link template open in another tab
- [ ] One peer-relevant case study queued up (not Tanya/Nadia for enterprise buyers)

During the call:
- [ ] Two-way fit language at the open, verbal yes from prospect before continuing
- [ ] Minimum four discovery questions with PIE layering
- [ ] Diagnose and confirm fit — explicit "would you agree?"
- [ ] Permission to pitch — explicit "does that sound good?"
- [ ] Pitch tied to what the prospect said, not generic
- [ ] Savings anchor stated with first mention of price
- [ ] Refundability stated with first mention of deposit
- [ ] Payment link sent mid-call if package is chosen
- [ ] Next step owned by us, not by the prospect

Red flags that mean you have NOT closed:
- "Send me the proposal"
- "Let me run a poll / ask my partner / speak to X this weekend"
- "I'll let you know"
- Your own next meeting ending the call
